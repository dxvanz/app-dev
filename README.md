 *Darling in the Franxx* is a Japanese mecha anime series produced by CloverWorks and Trigger. Set in a dystopian future, the series explores themes of identity, love, and survival as humanity battles giant monsters called *klaxosaurs*.

# **Main Characters in Darling in the Franxx**

## 1. **Hiro** (ヒロ)
- **Voiced by**: Natsuki Hanae
- **Role**: The protagonist and former promising pilot.
- **Key Traits**: Sensitive, compassionate, loyal.
- **Partner**: Zero Two.

## 2. **Zero Two** (ゼロツー)
- **Voiced by**: Mikako Komatsu
- **Role**: Half-human, half-klaxosaur girl; Hiro's partner.
- **Key Traits**: Fierce, passionate, independent, emotionally distant but caring towards Hiro.
- **Partner**: Hiro.

## 3. **Ichigo** (イチゴ)
- **Voiced by**: Aoi Yuuki
- **Role**: A fellow pilot who has feelings for Hiro.
- **Key Traits**: Mature, loyal, and somewhat possessive.
- **Partner**: Goro.

## 4. **Goro** (ゴロー)
- **Voiced by**: Takahiro Sakurai
- **Role**: Hiro's best friend and a fellow pilot.
- **Key Traits**: Reliable, caring, and stabilizing force.
- **Partner**: Ichigo.

## 5. **Miku** (ミク)
- **Voiced by**: Kana Ichinose
- **Role**: A sharp-tongued but caring pilot.
- **Key Traits**: Confident, sassy, protective.
- **Partner**: Zorome.

## 6. **Zorome** (ゾロメ)
- **Voiced by**: Kaito Ishikawa
- **Role**: A brash, comedic pilot.
- **Key Traits**: Energetic, impulsive, immature but sincere.
- **Partner**: Miku.

## 7. **Dr. Franks** (フランク博士)
- **Voiced by**: Toshiyuki Morikawa
- **Role**: The enigmatic scientist overseeing the Franxx program.
- **Key Traits**: Manipulative, calculating, secretive.

## 8. **Papa** (パパ)
- **Voiced by**: Unknown (only seen in hologram form)
- **Role**: Leader of the *Ape* organization.
- **Key Traits**: Authority figure, secretive, manipulative.

# **Supporting Characters**

## 9. **Kokoro** (ココロ)
- **Voiced by**: Ai Kayano
- **Role**: A kind, motherly figure in the pilot group.
- **Key Traits**: Gentle, caring, understanding.
- **Partner**: Futoshi.

## 10. **Futoshi** (フトシ)
- **Voiced by**: Shunsuke Takeuchi
- **Role**: Kokoro’s partner.
- **Key Traits**: Optimistic, kind, deeply caring.
- **Partner**: Kokoro.

# **Antagonists**

## The Klaxosaurs
- **Role**: Giant creatures from beneath the earth that serve as the primary threat to humanity.
- **Key Traits**: Mysterious, destructive, and the central conflict of the series.
